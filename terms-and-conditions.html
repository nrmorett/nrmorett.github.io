Program

Alfred is a personal assistant app run by Nicholas Moretton, a sole proprietor, for himself. It sends automated alerts (reminders, task updates, and messages from calls it took for him) from +1 (516) 207-0730 to one recipient only: the account holder's own mobile phone, after he opts in by texting START to that number. No marketing messages are sent, and no one else is messaged.
