Alfred Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Alfred is a personal assistant app run by an individual sole proprietor, Nicholas Moretton, for his own use. It sends automated text alerts from +1 (516) 207-0730 to one recipient only: the account holder's own mobile phone. It does not text anyone else, and there is no public sign-up.

How opting in works

The account holder enters his own mobile number in the Alfred app's settings, then texts START to +1 (516) 207-0730 from that phone. Alfred replies to confirm: "Alfred: You're subscribed to alerts from your Alfred assistant. Up to 10 msgs/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out." No other number can be added.

What we collect

Only the account holder's own phone number and the alerts sent to it.

How we use it

Only to send the account holder his own alerts: reminders, task updates, and messages from calls Alfred took for him. Phone numbers and message content are never sold, rented or shared with third parties, or used for marketing. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time and no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Security

The number is stored privately and is accessible only to the account holder and the messaging provider, Twilio.
