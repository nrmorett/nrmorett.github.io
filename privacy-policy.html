Alfred Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Alfred is a personal assistant operated by an individual sole proprietor. It sends low-volume, one-to-one text messages to personal contacts such as family and friends.

What we collect

We keep only the phone numbers and names of people who gave their number to the sender, and the messages exchanged with them.

How we use it

Information is used only to send and receive personal text messages with that person. We do not sell, rent or share phone numbers or message content with third parties or for marketing. Mobile opt-in data is never shared with third parties.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time and no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.

Security

Contact details are stored privately and are accessible only to the sender and the messaging provider, Twilio.
